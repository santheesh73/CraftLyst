# Lesson page for Stepwise, an evening-course platform

Stepwise is a fictional platform for adult evening courses. Course content is sample text.

## Who it is for

Adults studying after work on laptops and phones, often tired, who need to pick up where
they left off and read comfortably for 20 minutes.

## What to build

A static lesson page, `index.html` with CSS and minimal JS, no build step.

## Content

- Course "Introduction to Statistics", Module 3 "Spread and variability", Lesson 2
  "Standard deviation by hand".
- Progress: lesson 2 of 6 in this module, and a module outline with completed, current,
  and locked lessons.
- Lesson body (about 500 words of accurate teaching text): what standard deviation
  measures; a worked example with the data set 4, 8, 6, 5, 7 (mean 6, population standard
  deviation about 1.41); a formula block; and a "common mistake" callout about dividing by
  n versus n - 1.
- A three-question practice quiz with immediate feedback and an explanation per answer.
- Previous and Next lesson navigation and a "Mark complete" button.

## Constraints

- Long-form reading comfort: measure, line height, and type scale matter most.
- Math is readable without images (HTML and CSS, or MathML).
- Quiz feedback does not rely on colour alone and is announced to screen readers.
- Works from 320px to 1440px.
